Catto Learning is designed to collect only information needed to provide accounts, learning access, transactions, support and explicitly authorised research or marketing.

Core commitments

  • Personal information is not sold.
  • Marketing consent is separate from service administration.
  • Backup email addresses must be verified.
  • Users can inspect and revoke their active sessions.
  • Operational disclosures to payment, mail and hosting providers will be described accurately in the production privacy notice.
This Stage 1 page is a product-design statement, not the final legal privacy notice.
