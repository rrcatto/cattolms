Welcome

You do not need a farm, or even a garden, to grow good food. A balcony that gets five or six hours of sun, a strip of paving beside the kitchen, or a few square metres of yard can keep a household in herbs, leafy greens and tomatoes for much of the year. This course shows you how, step by step, in South African conditions.

Who this course is for

  • People who rent a flat or a small house and want to grow some of their own food.
  • Anyone who has tried a few pots of vegetables and watched them fail without knowing why.
  • Parents, community gardeners and schools who want a practical start.

You need no previous gardening experience. You do need a space that gets some direct sun, and a few hours a week.

What you will be able to do

By the end of the course you will be able to test and improve your soil, make your own compost, choose and fill the right container for each crop, plan what to plant in your region's seasons, and look after your plants until harvest.

How each module works

Every module follows the same path, and the outline shows how long each part takes:

  1. A short presentation that sets out what the module will let you do.
  2. A video, where a good one exists, showing the work being done.
  3. The lesson, the heart of the module, with worked examples and diagrams.
  4. A summary of the module on one screen.
  5. Flash cards to fix the key points in your memory.
  6. Practical work to do in your own space, with a model answer to check yourself against.
  7. A module assessment that counts towards your grade.

You can download the lesson, the summary, the flash cards and the practical work as PDFs to keep.
