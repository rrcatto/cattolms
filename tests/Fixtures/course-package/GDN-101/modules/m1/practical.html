These tasks put this module to work in your own space. By the end you will know your soil, have compost on the way, and have a container ready to plant in Module 2. You need a glass jar with a lid, a ruler, a bucket, and the space you plan to grow in. Check each task against the model answers when you have finished it.

Task 1: Do a jar test on your soil

Take soil from the place you would most like to grow vegetables, or from the yard if you will grow only in containers. Follow the six steps in section 2 of the lesson. Measure the depth of each layer after a day, and work out each one's share of the total.

What you produce: a labelled sketch of the jar, the depth and share of each layer, and one sentence saying which kind of soil you have and what you will do about it.

Time: 20 minutes, then a day for the jar to settle.

Task 2: Plan a compost bin for your household

For one week, keep a note of the greens your kitchen produces, measured roughly in buckets. Then plan where your bin or heap will go, what browns you will use and where you will get them, and how much of them you need each week.

What you produce: a half-page plan: the place, the container, your weekly greens, the browns you will add and how much, and how you will keep it damp and turned.

Time: 15 minutes a day for a week, then 30 minutes.

Task 3: Choose and fill one container

Choose one container for a crop you want to grow. Check it against the size table in section 5, make sure it drains, and work out how much potting soil it needs. Fill it, water it until water runs from the bottom, and mulch it.

What you produce: a note of the container's measurements and volume, the crop it is for, the potting soil you bought, and a photo of it filled and mulched.

Time: 45 minutes, plus a trip to buy potting soil.
